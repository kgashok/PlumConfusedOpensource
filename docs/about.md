# About Social for SaveSoil

Social for SaveSoil is a companion app for the SaveSoil movement. It helps you create and share posts about soil conservation and browse posts using the #SaveSoil hashtag.

## Share a post

- Sign in with your X (formerly Twitter) account to post or repost.
- Write a post of up to 280 characters, or attach an image.
- Unsent post text and selected images are saved as drafts in this browser.
- View your post history, refresh it, or delete a post.

## Explore SaveSoil posts

- Fetch recent #SaveSoil posts from X, or browse posts already saved by the app.
- Switch between all posts and original posts.
- Search posts by text or account name as you type.
- Repost a post from its card. A post you have already reposted is marked as unavailable to repost again.

## Get inspiration

Use the inspiration and funny-prompt tools, ask ChatGPT for a response, or create an image with OpenAI or xAI Imagine. Availability depends on the account and service access configured for the app.

## About the movement

SaveSoil is a global movement advocating for healthy soil and increased soil organic content. Learn more at [Conscious Planet](https://www.consciousplanet.org/soil).
